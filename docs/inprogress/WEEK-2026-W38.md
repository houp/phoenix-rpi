### 🔬 vkQuake lightmap regression — what is ruled OUT so far (no Pi time spent yet)

Following the regression-first rule: date it and diff the window before debugging. Window = 09-08 (good
clip) → 09-15 (bad clip). Cleared by reading, not guessing:

- **`r_gpulightmapupdate 1`** — forced since the port landed **2026-08-26**, so it was on for the *good*
  clip too. Not the change.
- **ports `bec6d0d` (drop the copysign stopgap)** — libphoenix's `copysign` is correct, and vkQuake
  calls it in exactly one place (`pr_edict.c` edict sorting). Not lighting.
- **mesa `274ee5abea9`** — GL/gallium path only; vkQuake does not go through it. It is also a *fix*.
- **the 09-11 V3D clock/reset family** — **zero** clock-retry, reset, wedge or timeout lines in today's
  vkQuake *and* QuakeSpasm UART logs (`clk=on`, first try). Nothing in that family fired.
- **the `#29` `imageExtent` rewrite** — its premise ("extent reaches the driver as 0x1x1") does not
  survive reading the code: `mipwidth` equals `glt->width` at that point. Harmless, but not the cause.

**Measurement correction:** "pure-black pixel coverage" does **not** separate the two clips — it reads
0.00% on both. The blocks are *dark*, not black. Cross-engine alignment against QuakeSpasm (the owner's
