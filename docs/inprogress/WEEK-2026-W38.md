### ★ vkQuake "lightmap regression" — ROOT CAUSE FOUND: it is a **different map**, not a render bug

**The two clips do not show the same level.** Straight from the UART logs:

| clip | `phoenix-demo.cfg` | demo | level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09-08 — *"the previous version looked better"* | `demo2` | demo2.dem | **the Grisly Grotto** (e1m2) |
| 09-15 — *"some lightmaps on walls are wrong"* | `demo1` | demo1.dem | **the Necropolis** (e1m3) |

e1m3 is one of the darkest, harshest-contrast levels id shipped — large unlit recesses with hard
straight boundaries against brightly-lit stone. That is exactly what "suddenly some specific walls are
too dark or too lit" describes, and it is what the map looks like **when rendered correctly**.

`id1/phoenix-demo.cfg` is **hand-staged — nothing generates it** — and it is the single switch that
picks the level. It got re-staged as `demo1` for this week's recordings; the 09-08 reel was captured
with `demo2`. So the change in appearance came from my own staging, not from the graphics stack.

**Fix for the reel: stage `demo2` and re-record vkQuake**, which puts back the exact level the owner
liked. ⚠ Stage it *after* a cycle's prepare step — the export's `usr/share` is re-synced from the
project rootfs-overlay, which silently removed the file between this afternoon's runs and now.
⚠ It must be **absent** for the #67 torch gate, which needs `map start`.

**✅ RESOLVED — and the renderer is exonerated by measurement, not by argument.** I re-recorded
vkQuake with `demo2` staged, so there is now a **same-engine, same-demo, same-map** pair one week
apart. The status bar is pixel-identical art at a given demo tick, which makes an exact frame anchor
(it failed across engines only because QuakeSpasm scales its HUD differently): **17 exact matches**.

⚠ Two measurement traps caught in the act while grading it, both of which had already produced a wrong
reading earlier today: a **frozen console tail** scores 4.10% on the seam metric on 27 consecutive
frames (white text on black is a luminance step with no colour step — a textbook false positive), and
an **ffmpeg contact sheet's last tile did not hold the time I computed for it**, which made a
prime-gameplay frame look like the console. Per-second scans of the decoded stream settled both; the
tile did not.

ⓘ Also worth knowing for the reel: **vkQuake plays ONE demo and drops to the console** (~60 s of
gameplay per run) because the glue issues a single `playdemo`; QuakeSpasm loops demo1→2→3. The
vkQuake clip therefore has a much shorter usable window than the others.

**What the metrics said** (kept, because they are why I did not stop at "looks different"): *two*
independent pixel metrics fail to separate the 09-08 and 09-15 clips — pure-black coverage reads
**0.00% on both**, and a lightmap-seam detector (strong relative luminance step with no matching
chromaticity step, which is what a wrong lightmap block looks like and a geometry edge does not) reads
median **0.102% vs 0.105%**. Two null results against an owner-visible difference meant the difference
was not in the pixels' *lighting* — it was in the *content*. The contact sheets then showed it at once.
