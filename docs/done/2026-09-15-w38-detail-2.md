### ✅ `vkq-tile-flicker` FIXED — vkQuake was rendering into the buffer being displayed

The one-frame black tile blocks were **not** a lightmap bug. vkQuake has no WSI swapchain, so it
rendered into a single VkImage backed by the fb0 pages and `storeOp=STORE` *was* the present — writing
the buffer the display was scanning out, while it was scanned out. V3D stores tiles one at a time, so a
refresh landing mid-store showed some tiles of the new frame and some of the old.

★ **Port-only fix — the winsys already had everything and the GL path already used it**, which is
exactly why QuakeSpasm was the clean control. vkQuake called `v3d_phoenix_set_scanout()` (records pa and
size) instead of `v3d_phoenix_scanout_init()` (probes the firmware's granted virtual height and sets up
the 2nd/3rd buffers), so `v3d_phoenix_flip()` was a **silent no-op** in that process. It now creates one
image/view/framebuffer per granted buffer, renders off-screen, and flips once the frame is complete.

**HW-verified:** `scanout init … virt_h=3240 -> 3 buffer(s) TRIPLE-BUFFER+page-flip`, three images bound
at distinct physical addresses, **0 faults**, and the render is unchanged (lum 27.4 vs 27.3, colours
1099 vs 1103, non-black 91.9% vs 91.5%). Escape hatch: `VKQ_SINGLE_BUFFER=1`.
⚠ **Honest limit: the flicker RATE is unmeasured.** The mechanism is gone by construction, but no
validated detector exists (7+ variants failed — they fire on fast camera motion or miss a frame
confirmed by eye), so I am not quoting a before/after number.

### 🐞 NEW BUG, and a correction to what I said above: `vkq-tile-flicker`

My "different map" finding stands for the *lighting*, but it was **not the whole report**. There is a
real, separate defect, and I verified it myself on three **consecutive frames from a single decode**:

**A single frame (1/30 s) shows hard-edged black rectangles; the frames immediately before and after,
at the same viewpoint, are clean.** Evidence: `artifacts/vkq-lightmap-dating/` (saved by the analysis)
plus my own `flicker3.png` triple.

**It is not a lightmap bug.** The blocks are axis-aligned in *screen* space, and one band crosses the
left wall, the arch recess, the right pillar and the right wall at identical screen rows — four
surfaces with independent lightmaps in texture space. Nothing lightmap-shaped can do that. V3D is a
**tile-based** renderer and the blocks are tile-shaped ⇒ suspect the **tile-store / present path**,
not the compute-lightmap shader. Prior art: [[project_pi4_quake_flicker_vcmbox]] (Quake flicker was
single-buffering).

**Control:** QuakeSpasm on the *same demo, same day, same capture card* is clean over 72/72
consecutive frames where vkQuake blocks. So it is not the grabber and not H.264.

⚠ **The rate is NOT yet measurable, and I am not quoting one.** Seven detector variants have now
failed (mine: three — two fire on fast camera motion, one misses the frame I confirmed by eye; the
analysis: four). Every verdict so far is contiguous-window visual review.
ⓘ Reported present on 09-08 at much lower amplitude, i.e. probably **pre-existing**, with amplitude or
rate having changed rather than the defect appearing — **I have not independently verified that half**.
ⓘ For the reel: the worst single-frame black spike inside the chosen vkQuake window (`112|22`) is the
camera passing a dark doorway, not a glitch. That is reassurance, not proof.

★ **ROOT-CAUSED the same day, from source, no Pi time:** vkQuake has **no WSI swapchain** — it renders
into ONE VkImage whose memory the winsys backs with the **fb0 physical pages**, and the render pass's
`storeOp=STORE` *is* the present. So **the GPU writes the buffer the display is scanning out, while it
is being scanned out**, and V3D stores tiles one at a time — a refresh landing mid-store shows some
tiles new and some still holding the previous frame. That is precisely a screen-space, tile-shaped,
one-frame artefact. ⚠ `v3d_phoenix_set_next_scanout()` is **not** a page flip despite the name (it
flags the next BO as scanout-backed so it gets RASTER tiling); vkQuake calls it once, at image
creation. **There is no per-frame flip in the Vulkan path at all.**

That also explains the control: the GL path **page-flips** (`scanout_nbuf` / `scanout_disp_off`), so
QuakeSpasm only ever displays completed frames. Same class as the earlier Quake dynamic-model flicker,
which was also single-buffering.

**Fix shape (not yet written):** give the Vulkan path the double-buffering the GL path already has —
render into the back buffer, then move the displayed offset. The winsys already detects whether a
second buffer was granted; what is missing is a per-frame flip entry point the Vulkan side can call.
Analysis: [`docs/misc/2026-09-15-vkq-tile-flicker-root-cause.md`](../misc/2026-09-15-vkq-tile-flicker-root-cause.md).
Cosmetic meanwhile, and absent from the reel's chosen vkQuake window.
