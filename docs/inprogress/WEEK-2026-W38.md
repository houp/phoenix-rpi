## 2c. ⏸ EDITORIAL — the reel opens with 62 s of text (your call)

Noticed while watching all 11 segments. The running order is Boot (17 s) → Shell (23 s) → Game of Life
(22 s) → X11 desktop. So the **first 62 s — 26% of a 239 s video — is monochrome text**, and the first
colour graphics arrive at t=62. For something published online, where viewers decide in the first
10–15 s, that is a long cold open.

It may well be deliberate: boot-first tells the "it genuinely boots on real hardware" story, and the
payoff order is nice. So I have **not** changed it. Two cheap alternatives if you want one — ~10 min
each, no re-recording, just a different segment table:

  underneath".
- **Trim the cold open:** Boot 17→10 s and Shell 23→14 s keeps the story and halves the text.
